```text
Who can author which authoritative fact?
Who can perform which transition?
Who can obtain which privileged capability?
```

```text

one mutable fact
→ one mutation authority family

one transition
→ one legal path

one privileged concrete authority
→ finite explicit composition owners

one consumer
→ minimum required capability only

unknown
→ fail closed
```

Do not optimize for the number of guards.

Optimize for a deterministic answer to:

```text
Who can change this fact?
```
