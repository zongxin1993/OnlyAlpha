5.2 Workload Plan invariants
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Validate the complete workload before execution.

At minimum enforce:

1. At least one calculation workload exists through Direct Job and/or Sweep.

   closure.

6. Every Statistics Plan target reference belongs to the workload calculation
   closure.

7. Statistics Plan fingerprints are duplicate-free.

8. Result Plan statistics fingerprints must EXACTLY equal the Statistics Plans
   supplied by the Workload Plan.

9. Cross-Dataset statistics/result composition is forbidden.

10. All exact references are validated before any new durable execution begins.

Validation must fail closed with stable formal error codes.
