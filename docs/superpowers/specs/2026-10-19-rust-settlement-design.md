# Rust Settlement authority

Settlement is a Domain-only semantic library for immutable, scoped cash-securities instructions, explicit versioned-calendar T+N schedules, six independent booking/availability/legal flags, deterministic due selection, maturity identity and atomic checkpoint recovery. It consumes a full Domain Calendar and compiled policy; concrete market policy stays Plugin-owned. Instruction identities bind complete economics, scope, policy, calendar and Position/Allocation lifecycle; no delimiter encoding or latest lookup.

Execution consumes opaque Settlement outputs and commits its checkpoint with the existing six frozen component groups. Registration follows the actual Fill and captures exact Position/Allocation identities/cycles. Maturity is its own operation, never a synthetic Broker Fill; recover installs frozen projections rather than evaluating a current calendar. New normalized source inputs and metadata evolve atomically, with no old-format fallback. Source hashes provide integrity, not authenticated producer authority; owning Runtime admission remains outside this non-activated library.

The library represents all six schedule axes. Existing cash Execution economically supports immediate asset/cash booking and immediate cash trading availability, with explicit T+N asset availability, cash withdrawal and legal settlement. Other economic shapes are explicitly rejected until their owning consumers support them; no date is silently treated as effective. Booking and cash trading transitions are still fully represented by the library. Withdrawal remains an availability fact, not a simulated external withdrawal.

Every maturity binds exact prior instruction version/hash, effective trading day, frozen Calendar and exact transition set; early, repeated, changed or wrong-scope effects cannot release twice. Position alone updates account/attribution buckets; Account alone releases proven receivables. Completed instructions and duplicate results survive recovery; registration limits never discard existing instructions or block their maturity. Python comparison covers correct common behavior; six-axis booking completion and strict restore/identity corrections are independently tested deviations rather than copied defects.

Formal Runtime activation, persistence drivers, deployment upgrade, LIVE, futures daily MTM and Shipping UI acceptance remain separate R3-R8 gates. Constitution impact is NO.

Resource admission reserves worst-case canonical Fill slots before submission, from exact Order quantity and Instrument quantity precision. Each positive Fill consumes at least one quantum, so existing instruction count plus all remaining quanta never increases on a Fill. Aggregate fills release unused future slots by that arithmetic proof. The original 512-instruction and 32 MiB checkpoint bounds remain; records and receipts have per-item bounds that reserve room for all six future transitions. No safety fact is admitted under an unreserved Order.
